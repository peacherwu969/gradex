Possible Failures

1.   System indicator/MIL stays on

Pre-check

1.   Battery fully charged

2.   Vehicle fuses are OK

3.   All connectors are OK, and there is no loose connector or pin corrosion

Inspection Tools

1.   Multimeter, scan tool

2.   Jumper box

1      ECU Power Supply Check
1.1   Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)
1.2   Connect the jumper box between ECU harness connector and ECU
1.3   Place the ignition switch in ON position
1.4   Measure the voltage of ECU harness connector pin to passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: 10~14V / Pin K01 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: 10~14V / Pin K03 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: 10~14V / Pin K05 to passenger compartment ground

 

Possible causes:

Fuse blown

Harness failure

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2      ECU Grounding Check
2.1   Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)
2.2   Pull out the ECU vehicle harness connector K
2.3   Measure the resistance between ECU harness connector pin and passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin K02 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin K04 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin K06 to passenger compartment ground

 

Possible causes:

Harness open-circuited

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)
