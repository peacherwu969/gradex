The following methods are applicable for electrical failure inspection. Pay attention to the following aspects during the troubleshooting process:

1      Check Battery and Grounding

Visually check the battery terminals for oxidation, good contact, poor ground contact, corrosion, rust, and electromagnetic interference.

2      Voltage Check and Harness Open Circuit or Short Circuit Check
2.1   Voltage check

   Place the ignition switch in position ON to power on the components to be checked

   Use a multimeter ("Voltage" position) to connect the negative pole to the passenger compartment ground, and connect the positive pole to the positive pole of the component

2.2   Harness check

When performing the harness check, be sure to disconnect the harness connector from the ECU and components. Connect the multimeter lead to the component pins

   Open circuit detection: Pull out ECU connector and component connector (e.g. temperature sensor or brake switch), and measure the resistance between relevant pins of the two connectors

   Short circuit detection: If the harness is short to ground, it can be determined by checking the grounding resistance.

3      Check Plug, Connector or Harness
3.1   Do not pull the harness when loosening or disconnecting the plug, but only pull the plug
3.2   Check the plug for deformation, damage or looseness when inserting the plug
3.3   When inserting the plug, press the connector firmly until it clamps in place (a ‘click' sound can be heard)
3.4   Make sure wires and plugs are reconnected
3.5   Do not twist or pull the harnesses
3.6   Make sure the harnesses are not stuck during the installation of components
3.7   Do not damage the protective cover of the harness
3.8   Failure case

4      Check ECU Connectors and Pins
4.1   Turn off the key switch

Note: After turning off the key switch, wait until the ECU is completely powered down before the removal (wait for more than 2 minutes)

4.2   Loosen the lock catch as shown in the figure, remove the ECU plug, and protect the plug effectively

4.3   Check ECU harness connector for corrosion, and check ECU pins for bending and corrosion

4.4   Insert ECU plug as shown below and secure the ECU lock catch (indicated by the red circle in the figure)
4.5   Secure the ECU harness; the distance between the first fixing point of the harness and the ECU connector should be within 10-15cm

5      Check Fuses or Fusible Elements
5.1   Open the main fuse box
5.2   Pull out the fuse of relevant circuit
5.3   Visually check if the fuse is blown
5.4   Check if the fuse is intact with a multimeter (measure the resistance). If the measured resistance is 0 ohm, it indicates that the fuse is normal.
6      Check Relays
6.1   Open the main fuse box
6.2   Visually check that all relays are installed in place
6.3   Measure the coil resistance with a multimeter
