Possible Failures

1.   System indicator/MIL stays on

2.   Low engine power

Pre-check

1.   Battery fully charged

2.   Vehicle fuses are OK

3.   All connectors are OK, and there is no loose connector or pin corrosion

4.   If necessary, drain off the water saved in the filter

Inspection Tools

1.   Multimeter, connector adapter (Y-type cable), scan tool

2.   Jumper box

1      Power Supply Check
1.1   Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)
1.2   Pull out the water-in-fuel sensor harness connector
1.3   Place the ignition switch in ON position
1.4   Measure the voltage of water-in-fuel sensor harness connector pin 3 to the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: 10~14V

Possible causes:

Damaged harness (make further troubleshooting through Step 2 "Harness Inspection")

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

Ignition switch damage

 
2      Harness Inspection
2.1   Open circuit detection

2.1.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.1.2    Pull out the water-in-fuel sensor harness connector

2.1.3    Pull out the ECU vehicle harness connector K

2.1.4    Pull out the main relay

2.1.5    Measure the resistance between the water-in-fuel sensor harness connector pin and relevant ECU pin

 

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 1 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 2 to ECU pin K70

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 3 to main relay pin 87

Possible causes:

Harness open-circuited

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2.2   Short-to-ground detection

2.2.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.2.2    Pull out the water-in-fuel sensor harness connector

2.2.3    Pull out the ECU vehicle harness connector K

2.2.4    Pull out the main relay

2.2.5    Measure the resistance between the water-in-fuel sensor harness connector pin 2/3 and the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ / Pin 2 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ / Pin 3 to passenger compartment ground

Possible causes:

Harness short to ground

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2.3   Harness internal short circuit detection

2.3.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.3.2    Pull out the water-in-fuel sensor harness connector

2.3.3    Pull out the ECU vehicle harness connector K

2.3.4    Pull out the main relay

2.3.5    Measure the resistance between the water-in-fuel sensor harness connector pin and all other pins of the harness connector

 

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 1 to all other pins

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 2 to all other pins

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 3 to all other pins

Possible causes:

Harness internal short circuit

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
3      Component Check
3.1   Water-in-fuel sensor air flow signal detection

3.1.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

3.1.2    Plug the water-in-fuel sensor harness connector

3.1.3    Plug the ECU harness connector

3.1.4    Plug the main relay

3.1.5    Place the ignition switch in ON position

3.1.6    Measure the waveform of the water-in-fuel sensor after just being powered on with an oscilloscope through a connector adapter (Y-type cable) or jumper box

Note:

The positive pole of oscilloscope is connected to water-in-fuel sensor pin 2 or ECU pin K70, the negative pole is connected to the passenger compartment ground
