Possible Failures

1.   System indicator/MIL stays on or flashes

2.   Low engine power

3.   Increased engine idle speed

4.   High fuel consumption

5.   Oil dilution

Pre-check

1.   Battery fully charged

2.   Vehicle fuses are OK

3.   All connectors are OK, and there is no loose connector or pin corrosion

Inspection Tools

1.   Multimeter, scan tool

2.   Jumper box

1      Power Supply Check
1.1   Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)
1.2   Pull out the T3 front boost temperature sensor harness connector
1.3   Place the ignition switch in ON position
1.4   Measure the voltage of the T3 front boost temperature sensor harness connector pin 2 to the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: 4.9 ~ 5.1V

Possible causes:

Damaged harness (make further troubleshooting through Step 2 "Harness Inspection")

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2      Harness Inspection
2.1   Open circuit detection

2.1.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.1.2    Pull out the ECU engine harness connector A

2.1.3    Pull out the T3 front boost temperature sensor harness connector

2.1.4    Measure the resistance between the T3 front boost temperature sensor harness connector pin and the ECU harness connector pin

 

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 1 to ECU pin A13

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 2 to ECU pin A12

Possible causes:

Harness open-circuited

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2.2   Short-to-ground detection

2.2.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.2.2    Pull out the ECU engine harness connector A

2.2.3    Pull out the T3 front boost temperature sensor harness connector

2.2.4    Measure the resistance between the T3 front boost temperature sensor harness connector pin and the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ / Pin 1 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ / Pin 2 to passenger compartment ground

Possible causes:

Harness short to ground

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2.3   Harness internal short circuit detection

2.3.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.3.2    Pull out the ECU engine harness connector A

2.3.3    Pull out the T3 front boost temperature sensor harness connector

2.3.4    Measure the resistance between the T3 front boost temperature sensor harness connector pin and all other pins of the connector

 

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 1 to all other pins

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 2 to all other pins

Possible causes:

Harness internal short circuit

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
3      Component Check
3.1   Resistance detection

3.1.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

3.1.2    Pull out the T3 front boost temperature sensor harness connector

3.1.3    Measure the resistance between pin 1 and pin 2 of the T3 front boost temperature sensor

 

Normal value: ~220 (at 5°C)

Possible causes:

Damaged T3 front boost temperature sensor

 
3.2   Signal voltage detection

3.2.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

3.2.2    Plug the ECU engine harness connector A

3.2.3    Plug the T3 front boost temperature sensor harness connector

3.2.4    Place the ignition switch in ON position

3.2.5    Read the data flow with the scan tool

 

Data flow

Temperature signal voltage

 

Normal value: ~0.835 V (at 0°C)

Normal value: ~1.081 V (at 100°C)

Normal value: ~1.293 V (at 200°C)

Normal value: ~1.640 V (at 400°C)
