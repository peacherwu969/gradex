1      Power Supply Check
1.1   Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)
1.2   Pull out the DPF differential pressure sensor connector
1.3   Place the ignition switch in ON position
1.4   Measure the voltage of DPF differential pressure sensor harness connector pin 1 to the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: 4.9 ~ 5.1V

 

Possible causes:

Damaged harness (make further troubleshooting through Step 2 "Harness Inspection")

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2      Harness Inspection
2.1   Open circuit detection

2.1.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.1.2    Pull out the ECU vehicle harness connector K

2.1.3    Pull out the DPF differential pressure sensor connector

2.1.4    Measure the resistance between the DPF differential pressure sensor harness connector pin and the ECU harness connector pin

 

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 1 to ECU pin K42

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 2 to ECU pin K15

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 3 to ECU pin K16

 

Possible causes:

Harness open-circuited

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2.2   Short-to-ground detection

2.2.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.2.2    Pull out the ECU vehicle harness connector K

2.2.3    Pull out the DPF differential pressure sensor connector

2.2.4    Measure the resistance between the DPF differential pressure sensor harness connector pin and the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ / Pin 1 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ / Pin 2 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ / Pin 3 to passenger compartment ground

 

Possible causes:

Harness short to ground

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2.3   Harness internal short circuit detection

2.3.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.3.2    Pull out the ECU vehicle harness connector K

2.3.3    Pull out the DPF differential pressure sensor connector

2.3.4    Measure the resistance between the DPF differential pressure harness connector pin and all other pins of the connector

 

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 1 to all other pins

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 2 to all other pins

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 3 to all other pins

 

Possible causes:

Harness internal short circuit

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
3      Component Check
3.1   DPF differential pressure signal voltage

3.1.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

3.1.2    Plug the ECU vehicle harness connector K

3.1.3    Plug the DPF differential pressure sensor harness connector

3.1.4    Place the ignition switch in ON position

3.1.5    Read the data flow with the scan tool

 

Data flow

DPF differential pressure (based on the soot mass and air intake flow in DPF)

 

Normal value: ~ 0 hPa (Engine shutdown)

Normal value: ~ 7 hPa (Idle speed: 750rpm)

Normal value: ~ 74 hPa (Max. speed: 3, 850rpm)

 

Note:

If the relevant signal value cannot be read from the scan tool, use the jumper box to directly measure the DPF differential pressure signal voltage.

 

DPF differential pressure signal voltage

Jumper box

ECU pin K59 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ~ 0.50 V (Engine shutdown)

Normal value: ~ 0.52 V (Idle speed: 750rpm)

Normal value: ~ 0.79 V (Max. speed: 3, 850rpm)

 

Possible causes:

Damaged DPF differential pressure sensor

 
3.2   Pressure pipe and joint detection

3.2.1    Check the two pressure pipes connecting DPF and the differential pressure sensor for leakage or blockage

3.2.2    Check if the joint between the pressure pipes and DPF and the differential pressure sensor is complete and if the connection is tight

3.2.3    Check the installation position of the pressure pipes. One is connected to the upstream of DPF, the other is connected to the downstream of DPF, and the two pressure pipes at the differential pressure sensor are not allowed to be reversed

 

 

 

Possible causes:

Pressure pipe leakage or blockage

Loosen pressure pipe joint

Positions of two pressure pipes reversed

 

 

End of test
