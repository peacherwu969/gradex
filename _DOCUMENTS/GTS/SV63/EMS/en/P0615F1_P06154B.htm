2.   Unable to start the engine

Pre-check

1.   Battery fully charged

2.   Vehicle fuses are OK

3.   All connectors are OK, and there is no loose connector or pin corrosion

Inspection Tools

1.   Multimeter, scan tool

2.   Jumper box

1      Power Supply Check
1.1   Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)
1.2   Pull out the starter relay
1.3   Place the ignition switch in ON position
1.4   Measure the voltage of starter relay socket pin 30, 86 to passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: 10~14V

Possible causes:

Damaged harness (make further troubleshooting through Step 2 "Harness Inspection")

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2      Harness Inspection
2.1   Open circuit detection

2.1.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.1.2    Pull out the ECU vehicle harness connector K

2.1.3    Pull out the starter relay

2.1.4    Measure the resistance between the starter relay socket pin 85 and ECU pin K13

2.1.5    Measure the resistance between the starter relay socket pin 86 and ECU pin K72

2.1.6    Measure the resistance between the starter relay socket pin 87 and the starter

2.1.7    Measure the resistance between the starter relay socket pin 30 and positive battery terminal

 

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Resistance between pin 85 and ECU pin K13

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Resistance between pin 86 and ECU pin K72

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Resistance between pin 87 and the starter

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Resistance between pin 30 and the positive battery terminal

Possible causes:

Harness open-circuited

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2.2   Short-to-ground detection

2.2.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.2.2    Pull out the ECU vehicle harness connector K

2.2.3    Pull out the starter relay

2.2.4    Measure the resistance between the starter relay socket pin and the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 85 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 86 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 87 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 30 to passenger compartment ground

Possible causes:

Harness short to ground

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2.3   Harness internal short circuit detection

2.3.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.3.2    Pull out the ECU vehicle harness connector K

2.3.3    Pull out the starter relay

2.3.4    Measure the resistance between the starter relay socket pin and all other pins in the relay socket

 

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 85 to all other pins

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 86 to all other pins

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 87 to all other pins

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 30 to all other pins

Possible causes:

Harness internal short circuit

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
3      Component Check
3.1   Resistance detection: starter relay

3.1.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

3.1.2    Pull out the starter relay

3.1.3    Measure the resistance between the starter relay pin 85 and pin 86

 

Normal value: 80~100Ω

Possible causes:

Damaged starter relay

 
3.2   ECU: Starter relay control signal open circuit voltage

3.2.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

3.2.2    Plug the ECU vehicle harness connector K

3.2.3    Pull out the starter relay

3.2.4    Place the ignition switch in ON position

3.2.5    Measure the voltage of ECU pin K72/K18 to passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: 10~3.5V / Voltage of K18 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: 10~2.5V / Voltage of K72 to passenger compartment ground

Possible causes:

Poor contact between ECU harness connector and ECU

Incorrect ECU data

ECU damage
