Possible Failures

1.   System indicator/MIL stays on

2.   Low engine power

3.   Hard to start the engine

4.   Unable to start the engine

5.   Engine idle wobbling

6.   Increased engine idle speed

7.   High fuel consumption

8.   Oil dilution

Pre-check

1.   Battery fully charged

2.   Vehicle fuses are OK

3.   All connectors are OK, and there is no loose connector or pin corrosion

Inspection Tools

1.   Multimeter, scan tool

2.   Jumper box

Note:

The engine firing order is 1-3-4-2.

1      Harness Inspection
1.1   Open circuit detection

1.1.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

1.1.2    Pull out the ECU engine harness connector A

1.1.3    Pull out the harness connector from all fuel injectors

1.1.4    Measure the resistance between the fuel injector harness connector pin and the relevant ECU harness connector pin

 

Fuel injector 1:

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 1 to pin A64

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 2 to pin A85

Fuel injector 2:

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 1 to pin A24

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 2 to pin A45

Fuel injector 3:

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 1 to pin A22

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 2 to pin A43

Fuel injector 4:

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 1 to pin A66

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 2 to pin A87

Possible causes:

Harness open-circuited

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
1.2   Short-to-ground detection

1.2.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

1.2.2    Pull out the ECU engine harness connector A

1.2.3    Disconnect the harness connector from fuel injector [n]

1.2.4    Measure the resistance between fuel injector [n] harness connector pin 1/2 and the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ / Pin 1 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ / Pin 2 to passenger compartment ground

Possible causes:

Harness short to ground

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
1.3   Short-to-battery detection

1.3.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

1.3.2    Pull out the ECU engine harness connector A

1.3.3    Disconnect the harness connector from fuel injector [n]

1.3.4    Place the ignition switch in ON position

1.3.5    Measure the voltage between fuel injector [n] harness connector pin 1/2 and the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: ~0V / Pin 1 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ~0V / Pin 2 to passenger compartment ground

Possible causes:

Harness short to battery

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
1.4   Harness internal short circuit detection

1.4.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

1.4.2    Pull out the ECU engine harness connector A

1.4.3    Disconnect the harness connector from fuel injector [n]

1.4.4    Measure the resistance between fuel injector [n] harness connector pin 1 and pin 2

 

Normal value: ~ 1MΩ / Pin 1 to pin 2

Possible causes:

Harness high side short to low side

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2      Component Check
2.1   Resistance detection

2.1.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.1.2    Disconnect the harness connector from fuel injector [n]

2.1.3    Measure the resistance between fuel injector [n] pin 1 and pin 2

 

Normal value: 0.2~1Ω / Pin 1 to pin 2

Possible causes:

Damaged fuel injector

 

End of test
