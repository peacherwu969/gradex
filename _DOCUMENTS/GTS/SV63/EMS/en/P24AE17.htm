3. All connectors are OK, and there is no loose connector or pin corrosion

1      Power Supply Check
1.1   Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)
1.2   Pull out the PM sensor harness connector
1.3   Place the ignition switch in ON position
1.4   Measure the voltage of PM sensor harness connector pin 1 to the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: 10~14V

Possible causes:

Damaged harness (make further troubleshooting through Step 2 "Harness Inspection")

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

ECU damage

 
2      Harness Inspection
2.1   Open circuit check

2.1.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.1.2    Pull out the ECU engine harness connector A

2.1.3    Pull out the urea supply unit harness connector 1

2.1.4    Measure the resistance between urea supply unit harness connector 1 pin and ECU engine harness connector A pin

 

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 1 to positive battery terminal

Normal value: ~0Ω / Pin 2 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ~0Ω / Pin 3 to ECU pin K81

Normal value: ~0Ω / Pin 4 to ECU pin K80

Possible causes:

Harness open-circuited

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2.2   Short-to-ground detection

2.2.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.2.2    Pull out the ECU harness connector K

2.2.3    Pull out the PM sensor

2.2.4    Measure the resistance between the PM sensor pin and the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ / Pin 3 to K81

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ / Pin 4 to K80

Possible causes:

Harness short to ground

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2.3   Harness internal short circuit detection

2.3.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.3.2    Pull out the ECU vehicle harness connector K

2.3.3    Pull out the PM sensor

2.3.4    Measure the resistance between the PM sensor pin and all other pins of the connector

 

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 1 to all other pins

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 2 to all other pins

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 3 to all other pins

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 4 to all other pins
2.4   Short-to-ground detection

2.4.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.4.2    Pull out the ECU harness connector K

2.4.3    Pull out the PM sensor

2.4.4    Measure the voltage between the PM sensor pin and the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ / Pin 1 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ / Pin 3 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ / Pin 4 to passenger compartment ground

Possible causes:

Harness short to ground

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
3      Component Check
3.1   Resistance detection

3.1.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

3.1.2    Pull out the PM sensor

3.1.3    Measure the resistance between PM sensor pin 3 and pin 4

 

Normal value: ~60 Ω

Possible causes:

Damaged connector

 
3.2   Visual inspection

3.1.1    Remove the sensor.

3.1.2    Check if the probe is damaged.

Possible causes:

Damaged sensor
