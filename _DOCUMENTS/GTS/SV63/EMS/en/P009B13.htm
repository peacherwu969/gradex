Possible Failures

1.   System indicator/MIL stays on

2.   Engine idle wobbling

3.   Hard to start the engine

4.   Low engine power

5.   Unable to start the engine

Pre-check

1.   Battery fully charged

2.   Vehicle fuses are OK

3.   All connectors are OK, and there is no loose connector or pin corrosion

Inspection Tools

1.   Multimeter, connector adapter (Y-type cable), scan tool

2.   Jumper box

1      Power Supply Check
1.1   Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)
1.2   Pull out the rail pressure control valve harness connector
1.3   Place the ignition switch in ON position
1.4   Measure the voltage of the rail pressure control valve harness connector pin 1 to the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: 10~14V

Possible causes:

Damaged harness (make further troubleshooting through Step 2 "Harness Inspection")

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2      Harness Inspection
2.1   Open circuit detection

2.1.6    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.1.7    Pull out the rail pressure control valve harness connector

2.1.8    Pull out the main relay

2.1.9    Pull out the ECU engine harness connector A

2.1.10  Measure the resistance between the rail pressure control valve harness connector pin and the ECU harness connector pin

 

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 1 to ECU pin A27

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 2 to ECU pin A26

Possible causes:

Harness open-circuited

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2.2   Short-to-ground detection

2.2.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.2.2    Pull out the rail pressure control valve harness connector

2.2.3    Pull out the main relay

2.2.4    Pull out the ECU engine harness connector A

2.2.5    Measure the resistance between the rail pressure control valve harness connector pin and the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ / Pin 1 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ / Pin 2 to passenger compartment ground

Possible causes:

Harness short to ground

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2.3   Harness internal short circuit detection

2.3.6    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.3.7    Pull out the rail pressure control valve harness connector

2.3.8    Pull out the main relay

2.3.9    Pull out the ECU engine harness connector A

2.3.10  Measure the resistance between pin 1 and pin 2of the rail pressure control valve harness connector

 

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ

Possible causes:

Harness internal short circuit

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
3      Component Check
3.1   Resistance detection: rail pressure control valve

3.1.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

3.1.2    Pull out the rail pressure control valve harness connector

3.1.3    Directly measure the resistance between rail pressure control valve pin 1 and pin 2

 

Normal value: 4.0~5.0Ω

Possible causes:

Damaged rail pressure control valve

 
3.2   ECU: Rail pressure control valve control signal open circuit voltage detection

3.2.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

3.2.2    Pull out the rail pressure control valve harness connector

3.2.3    Plug the ECU engine harness connector A

3.2.4    Plug the main relay

3.2.5    Place the ignition switch in ON position

3.2.6    Measure the voltage of the rail pressure control valve harness connector pin 1 to the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: 3.0~4.0V

Possible causes:

Poor contact between ECU harness connector and ECU

Incorrect ECU data

ECU damage
