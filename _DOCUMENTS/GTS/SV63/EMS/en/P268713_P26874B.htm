1.   System indicator/MIL stays on

2.   Low engine power

3.   Unable to start the engine

Pre-check

1.   Battery fully charged

2.   Vehicle fuses are OK

3.   All connectors are OK, and there is no loose connector or pin corrosion

Inspection Tools

1.   Multimeter, scan tool

2.   Jumper box

1      Power Supply Check
1.1   Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)
1.2   Pull out the fuel heater relay
1.3   Place the ignition switch in ON position
1.4   Measure the voltage of fuel heater relay socket pin to passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: 10~14V / Fuel heater relay socket pin 2 to passenger compartment ground

Possible causes:

Damaged harness (make further troubleshooting through Step 2 "Harness Inspection")

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2      Harness Inspection
2.1   Open circuit detection

2.1.1  Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.1.2  Pull out the ECU vehicle harness connector K

2.1.3  Pull out the fuel heater relay

2.1.4  Pull out the main relay

2.1.5  Measure the resistance between the fuel heater relay socket pin 2 and the main relay socket pin 87

2.1.6  Measure the resistance between the fuel heater relay socket pin 1 and ECU pin K20

 

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Fuel heater relay socket pin 2 to main relay socket pin

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Fuel heater relay socket pin 1 to ECU pin K20

Possible causes:

Harness open-circuited

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2.2   Short-to-ground detection

2.2.1  Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.2.2  Pull out the ECU vehicle harness connector K

2.2.3  Pull out the fuel heater relay

2.2.4  Pull out the main relay

2.2.5  Measure the resistance between the fuel heater relay socket pin 1 and the pin to the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ /

Possible causes:

Harness short to ground

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2.3   Harness internal short circuit detection

2.3.1  Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.3.2  Pull out the ECU vehicle harness connector K

2.3.3  Pull out the fuel heater relay

2.3.4  Pull out the main relay

2.3.5  Measure the resistance between the fuel heater relay socket pin and all other pins in the relay socket

 

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Relay socket pin 1 to all other pins

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Relay socket pin 2 to all other pins

Possible causes:

Harness internal short circuit

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
3      Component Check
3.3   Resistance detection:

3.3.1  Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

3.3.2  Pull out the relay

3.3.3  Measure the resistance between the relay pin 1 and pin 2

 

Normal value: 90~110Ω

Possible causes:

Damaged relay

 
3.4   ECU: Relay control signal open circuit voltage

3.4.1  Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

3.4.2  Pull out the relay

3.4.3  Plug the ECU vehicle harness connector K

3.4.4  Plug the main relay

3.4.5  Place the ignition switch in ON position

3.4.6  Measure the voltage of ECU pin K20 to passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: 3.0~4.0V

Possible causes:

Poor contact between ECU harness connector and ECU

Incorrect ECU data

ECU damage
