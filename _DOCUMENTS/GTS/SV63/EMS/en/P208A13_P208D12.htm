2      Power Supply Check
2.1   Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)
2.2   Pull out the urea supply unit harness connector 1
2.3   Place the ignition switch in ON position
2.4   Measure the voltage of the urea supply unit harness connector pin 4/1 to the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: 10~14V

Possible causes:

Damaged harness (make further troubleshooting through Step 2 "Harness Inspection")

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

ECU damage

 
3      Harness Inspection
3.1   Open circuit check

3.1.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

3.1.2    Pull out the ECU engine harness connector A

3.1.3    Pull out the urea supply unit harness connector 1

3.1.4    Measure the resistance between urea supply unit harness connector 1 pin and ECU engine harness connector A pin

 

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 1 to SCR main relay 5

Normal value: ~0Ω / Pin 2 to ECU pin A05

Normal value: ~0Ω / Pin 3 to ECU pin A92

Normal value: ~0Ω / Pin 4 to ECU pin A89

Possible causes:

Harness open-circuited

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
3.2   Short-to-ground detection

3.2.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

3.2.2    Pull out the ECU engine harness connector A

3.2.3    Pull out the urea supply unit harness connector 1

3.2.4    Measure the resistance between the urea supply unit harness connector 1 pin and the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ / Pin 3 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ / Pin 4 to passenger compartment ground

Possible causes:

Harness short to ground

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
3.3   Harness internal short circuit detection

3.3.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

3.3.2    Pull out the ECU vehicle harness connector A

3.3.3    Pull out the urea supply unit harness connector 1

3.3.4    Measure the resistance between the urea supply unit harness connector 1 pin and all other pins of the connector

 

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 1 to all other pins

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 2 to all other pins

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 3 to all other pins

Normal value: ≥ 1 MΩ / Pin 4 to all other pins
3.4   Short-to-battery detection

3.4.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

3.4.2    Pull out the ECU engine harness connector A

3.4.3    Pull out the urea supply unit harness connector 1

3.4.4    Measure the voltage between the urea supply unit harness connector 1 pin and the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: ~0V / Pin 3 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ~0V / Pin 4 to passenger compartment ground

Possible causes:

Harness short to ground

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
4      Component Check
4.1   Resistance detection

4.1.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

4.1.2    Pull out the oil urea supply unit connector 1

4.1.3    Directly measure the resistance between pin 3 and pin 4 of the urea supply unit connector 1

 

Normal value: 3.5~4.5Ω / 25°C

Possible causes:

Damaged urea pump
