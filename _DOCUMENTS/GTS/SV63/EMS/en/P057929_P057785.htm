2.   Vehicle fuses are OK

3.   All connectors are OK, and there is no loose connector or pin corrosion

1      Power Supply Check
1.1   Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)
1.2   Pull out the cruise switch harness connector
1.3   Place the ignition switch in ON position
1.4   Measure the voltage of the harness connector pin 1 to the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: 10~14V / Pin 1 to passenger compartment ground

 

Possible causes:

Damaged harness (make further troubleshooting through Step 2 "Harness Inspection")

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2      Harness Inspection
2.1   Open circuit detection

2.1.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.1.2    Pull out the ECU vehicle harness connector K

2.1.3    Pull out the cruise switch harness connector

2.1.4    Pull out the main relay

2.1.5    Measure the resistance between the harness connector pin and ECU vehicle harness connector K pin

 

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 1 to ECU pin K63

Normal value: ~ 0Ω / Pin 2 to ECU pin K10

 

Possible causes:

Harness open-circuited

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
2.2   Short-to-ground detection

2.2.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 2 minutes before proceeding to avoid hot plugging)

2.2.2    Pull out the ECU vehicle harness connector K

2.2.3    Pull out the cruise switch harness connector

2.2.4    Measure the resistance between the harness connector pin and the passenger compartment ground

 

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ / Pin 1 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ≥ 1MΩ / Pin 2 to passenger compartment ground

 

Possible causes:

Harness short to ground

Damaged connector (visually check if the connector is intact)

 
3      Component Test
3.1   Signal voltage detection

3.1.1    Place the ignition switch in OFF position (if the last status of the ignition switch is ON, then after placing it in OFF position, please wait at least 60 seconds before proceeding)

3.1.2    Plug the ECU vehicle harness connector

3.1.3    Plug the cruise control switch harness connector

3.1.4    Place the ignition switch in ON position

3.1.5    Read the data flow with the scan tool:

Data flow

Normal value:                        Press       (Press the cruise switch)

                         Release   (Release the cruise switch)

 

Note:

If the relevant signal value cannot be read from the scan tool, use the jumper box to directly measure the voltage value of relevant pin of the cruise switch.

 

Cruise control switch signal voltage

Jumper box

ECU pin K63 to passenger compartment ground

Normal value: ~ 1.59V (Cruise Reset Switch)

Normal value: ~ 2.54V (Cruise OFF Switch)

Normal value: ~ 4.00V (Cruise Acceleration Switch)

Normal value: ~ 3.10V (Cruise Deceleration Switch)

 

Possible causes:

Damaged cruise control switch
